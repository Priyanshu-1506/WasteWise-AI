The Basics

What exactly is
waste management?

Waste management is the process of reducing, collecting, separating, treating, recycling and safely disposing of waste so that it causes less harm to people and the planet.

🛍️

Reduce

Avoid creating unnecessary waste in the first place.

🔄

Reuse

Use products again instead of throwing them away.

♻️

Recycle

Send suitable materials into recycling systems.

🍌

Compost

Organic waste can become useful compost.

🗑️

Dispose

Waste that cannot be recovered should be safely disposed.

Learn To Separate

Not all waste is the same.

Understanding waste categories is one of the simplest steps toward better waste management.

🍌

Wet / Organic

Food scraps, vegetable peels, fruit waste and other biodegradable materials.

📦

Dry / Recyclable

Paper, cardboard, metal and suitable recyclable materials.

🔋

E-Waste

Batteries, electronic devices and other electronic waste need special handling.

🗑️

Reject Waste

Waste that cannot reasonably be recycled or composted through available systems.

Think Again

Waste myths vs reality.

Good waste management starts with understanding what is actually true.

❌ MYTH

"Waste disappears after I throw it away."


✅ FACT

Throwing something away only moves it out of your sight. It still needs collection, processing or disposal.

❌ MYTH

"Everything made of plastic can be recycled."


✅ FACT

Different plastics have different recycling possibilities. Local recycling systems determine what can actually be recovered.

❌ MYTH

"One person cannot make a difference."


✅ FACT

Individual habits influence how much waste is created and whether materials remain recoverable.

❌ MYTH

"Waste management is only the government's job."


✅ FACT

Governments and waste workers provide essential systems, but responsible waste generation and segregation starts with citizens.
